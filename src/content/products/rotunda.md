Rotunda is a video player built for PC VR. It plays 360° video that surrounds you, 180° video that fills your view, and ordinary flat video on a screen in front of you. Each of these works in mono or in stereo.

Rotunda is built in Unity and decodes video with its own FFmpeg-based decoders.

Rotunda is in development for Windows PC VR, with Steam and the Meta Horizon Store as the planned stores.
