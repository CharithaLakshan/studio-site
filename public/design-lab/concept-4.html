M. Charitha Lakshan (“Lucky”)

Immersive Experience Engineer · final-year Computer Science, UCSC, Sri Lanka

Abstract

[LKY] is a one-person studio from Sri Lanka. Its main interest is computer graphics and simulation: renderers, real-time graphics, simulation and procedural generation. Game development comes second and XR experiences third. This page presents the studio’s first product, Rotunda, a PC VR video player (§2); its lab, including Kirana, a renderer written from scratch, and a 2026 journal paper on machine learning for 3D tree branching structures (§3); and an index of products (§4). Figure 1 is running in your browser.

Keywords: real-time rendering, simulation, procedural generation, games, XR

1Introduction

The studio is run by one person whose day job is XR. The studio itself is ordered differently. Its priorities, in order, are:

  1. computer graphics and simulation: rendering, real-time graphics, simulation and procedural generation;
  2. game development;
  3. XR experiences.

The work starts at the renderer and moves outwards. Products are released as they are ready, and each one has a permanent page with links to its stores.

2Featured: Rotunda

Rotunda is a VR video player for Windows PC VR. It plays 360° video that surrounds the viewer, 180° video that fills the view, and flat video on a screen in front of the viewer, each in mono or stereo. It is built in Unity and decodes video with its own FFmpeg-based decoders. It is in development, with Steam and the Meta Horizon Store as the planned stores.

3.1Kirana, a renderer from scratch

Kirana (working name) is a renderer written from scratch in C++, in a C style. It is a CPU software renderer first; a Vulkan port comes later. Pipeline stages, for example anti-aliasing modes, can be swapped while it runs. Ray and path tracing visualisation is planned. It is in early development.

For reference, Eq. (1) is the rendering equation [3], the integral that a path tracer estimates:

Lo(x,ωo) = Le(x,ωo) + ∫Ω fr(x,ωi,ωo) Li(x,ωi) (ωi·n) dωi (1)

3.2Research: trees

The studio’s research area is procedural and machine-learning-based 3D tree generation. The first-author paper is [1]:

M. Charitha Lakshan, K. Damitha Sandaruwan and L. N. C. De Silva. “A Behavioral Analysis of Machine Learning for Intrinsic 3D Tree Branching Structures.” IJACSA, Vol. 17, No. 7, 2026. doi:10.14569/IJACSA.2026.0170783
