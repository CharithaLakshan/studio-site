Five graphics-first home pages

Each concept is one self-contained HTML file with a real-time element written in vanilla WebGL2 or Canvas 2D. All product art is placeholder. Press ` on any concept for a frame-time overlay. The full write-up, ranking and recommendation are in Docs/Design/Exploration/Concepts.md.

  1. 1 · Key Light, Relit
