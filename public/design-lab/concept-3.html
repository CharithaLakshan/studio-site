A studio built around computer graphics and simulation: renderers, real-time graphics and procedural generation. The games and XR experiences come out of that work.

0000spp

Live path tracer in WebGL2. Every frame adds one sample per pixel. At 1024 samples it stops and the GPU goes idle. Click, drag or use the arrow keys on the image to move the light: the noise comes back, then converges again.
